First release published on F-Droid.

- The Android build is now split into two flavors. "full" is unchanged and keeps the optional sherpa-onnx speech engine; "fdroid" is compiled entirely from source and uses the Whisper on-device engine.
- A release build no longer fails when no signing keystore is configured; it produces an unsigned APK instead.
- Store listing text, icon and changelogs are now kept in the repository.
